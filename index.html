About CRAIL

Canadian Review of Artificial Intelligence, Law & Society (CRAIL) is an independent Canadian interdisciplinary academic journal devoted to the legal, social, institutional, ethical and philosophical implications of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

CRAIL provides a forum for scholars, legal practitioners, technologists and other professionals examining the relationship between artificial intelligence, law and society. The journal welcomes original research articles, doctrinal and interdisciplinary studies, essays, case analyses and critical commentary addressing AI governance, legal responsibility, human judgment, accountability, regulation, technology and society.

CRAIL is committed to academic independence, intellectual rigor, transparency and meaningful human responsibility in scholarly research and publication.

For Authors

Publication Fees — Founding Issues

Full publication-fee waiver for CRAIL's first three numbered issues (Vol. 1, Nos. 1–3).

CRAIL does not charge manuscript submission fees or peer-review fees.

Beginning with the fourth issue, the standard publication fee is US$1,000 per accepted article, plus applicable taxes, if any. Payment is required only after formal acceptance.

The Editorial Office may grant partial reductions or full waivers based on the significance of the scholarly contribution. All editorial decisions are independent of financial considerations.

Read the Complete Editorial, Peer Review and Publication Policies (PDF)

CRAIL welcomes original scholarly contributions addressing the legal, social, institutional, ethical and philosophical dimensions of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

Submissions: CRAIL considers research articles, doctrinal and interdisciplinary studies, essays, case analyses and critical commentary. Manuscripts should be original, clearly structured and supported by appropriate scholarly sources.

Peer Review: Scholarly submissions are subject to editorial assessment and, where appropriate, independent peer review by qualified specialists in the relevant field.

Academic Integrity: Authors remain responsible for the accuracy of their work, including factual claims, quotations, citations and references. Any use of artificial intelligence tools in research or manuscript preparation should be disclosed where materially relevant.

Submission Guidelines: Detailed manuscript formatting and submission instructions will be published shortly.
