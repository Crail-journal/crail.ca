Abstract

This article presents a selective critical review of AI governance scholarship circulated through the SSRN Legal Scholarship Network during September 2026. From a corpus of more than one hundred research entries identified in the monitored SSRN alerts, the author selects a limited number of works considered particularly significant for their legal arguments, competing doctrinal approaches, methodological implications, and potential contributions to the development of AI governance. The review is deliberately selective and does not purport to provide an exhaustive survey of SSRN publications or AI governance scholarship beyond the specified period.
